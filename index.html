about me ✎

Hi, I'm Prajwal

Engineering was never my dream. I started in Electrical Engineering, switched to Artificial Intelligence in my second year, and a game-development workshop in my 4th semester changed everything.

I was picked from around 180 students for a game-dev internship and learned Unity by shipping: a racing game first, then a puzzle game, then more. Now I make small, fun games you can play in a browser tab and teach students to make their own.

room 03 Projects

room 03 · projects · boss level

IFERTS

Industrial Fire Emergency Response Training Simulator

A 3D fire-safety trainer that runs in an ordinary web browser, built with Three.js and WebGL. You walk through a virtual factory and handle an electrical (Class C) fire from start to finish. No headset needed, and it's built so VR/XR can be added later.

Play the live demo →

The mission, step by step

  1. Wear PPE
  2. Pick the right extinguisher
  3. Pull · Aim · Squeeze · Sweep
  4. Put out the fire
  5. Raise the alarm
  6. Shut down machines
  7. Evacuate

A finite-state machine keeps every step in order, an event-driven design connects the modules, and every procedural mistake gets logged.

research paper · submitted

IFERTS: Industrial Fire Emergency Response Training Simulator, a Web-Based 3D Interactive Training System with VR/XR Readiness

Submitted to ICETEMS 2027, results awaited. Team: Saloni Ramteke, Shravani Shinde, Srushti Kature, Siddhant Fulzele & Prajwal Ghagre, Dept. of AI, G H Raisoni College of Engineering & Management.
